Refund Processing SOP

Source: Master Guide, Module 8 and Flow 8. Procedures are kept separate by system.

Checkout Champ / Konnektive

StepAction
1Customer > Customer Service > Search Orders (email, Order ID, phone or full name)
2Open customer profile, then click the Order ID for the Order Summary
3Actions panel: Full Refund or Partial Refund; enter exact amount for partial
4Choose Refund Reason; check Cancel Order After Refund? if applicable; Submit Refund
5System tries a Void first; if settled it processes a Refund
Manual: Pending/AuthorizedCustomer Profile > Transactions > Actions > Void (0-48 hours). Partial voids not supported
Manual: Settled/CompleteActions > Refund with amount (7-14 business days)
Refunded outside CRMCheck Refunded Externally before submitting
DocumentHistory > green + Note: reason, amount, resolution

Shopify

Source does not say when to use Shopify instead of Checkout Champ.

StepAction
1Shopify Admin or mobile app > Orders > open the order > Refund
FullEnter full quantities; confirm Restock; enter shipping to refund if applicable; optional reason; choose method (original payment or store credit); confirm customer notification; click Refund $[Amount]
PartialUse Line Item (adjust quantities, others 0) or Dollar Amount (Summary field); adjust shipping/discounts; choose method; notification choice; click Refund $[Amount]
